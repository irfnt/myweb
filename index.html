About Me

I hold a Ph.D. in Information and Communication Engineering with research expertise in advanced signal processing, sparse signal recovery, machine learning, radio astronomy systems, and FPGA-based real-time architectures. My work integrates algorithm design with hardware implementation for high-performance intelligent systems.

Currently, I am a Staff Engineer specializing in Clock Tree Synthesis (CTS), timing closure, and Power-Performance-Area (PPA) optimization at advanced semiconductor nodes (3nm/5nm). I am actively seeking postdoctoral opportunities in signal processing, AI-driven systems, hardware-aware machine learning, and intelligent sensing technologies.
